If your income stopped tomorrow,
what would keep going?

This is not a question meant to scare you.
It is a question meant to organize.

  • Does the house keep going?

    Rent or mortgage, bills, upkeep and everything else that keeps the routine running.

  • Do the kids carry on?

    School, college, activities and opportunities you do not want disappearing because of one turn nobody saw coming.

  • Do the plans stay?

    Trips, retirement, a business, a move, dreams that have not happened yet but are already part of your life together.

  • Is there time?

    Protection also means buying time, so your family does not have to make urgent decisions at the worst possible moment.

Insurance is not about expecting the worst.
It is about protecting what is already going right.

There is life insurance you do not have to die to use.

Some policies come with features called Living Benefits. If you are diagnosed with a serious condition, you can draw part of your own benefit while you are still living and use it however you need: treatment, the bills at home, the kids at school, whatever it takes.

  • Terminal illness

    When life expectancy drops to one or two years.

  • Chronic illness

    When you can no longer perform two of six basic daily activities, or there is severe cognitive loss.

  • Critical illness

    Cancer, heart attack and stroke are among the conditions usually on the list.

  • Critical injury

    Paralysis, coma, severe burns, serious brain injury.
